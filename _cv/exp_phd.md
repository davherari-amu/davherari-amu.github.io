details:
    - "Developed a bio-inspired generative-design method for contact interfaces that, unlike shape optimisation, does not need a starting shape close to the final one (Python, Code_Aster, Gmsh)."
    - "Generated interfaces with up to 35% more uniform pressure under static loads, and up to 73% lower peak shear stress under time-dependent loads."
    - "Built response-surface metamodels from full-factorial designs of experiments to quantify how design parameters drive pressure uniformity."
    - "Developed a wear-simulation framework (Archard's law, Code_Aster) predicting about 10% less maximum wear for a 3D-printed bio-inspired joint than for a cylindrical one."
    - "Co-supervised a Master's internship on the wear of mechanical joints."
